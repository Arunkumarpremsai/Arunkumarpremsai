# 👋 About Me

AI Engineer with a **Post Graduate Program in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning from Purdue University**, passionate about building intelligent solutions using **Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and MLOps**.

I enjoy developing end-to-end AI applications ranging from **Recommendation Systems**, **RAG pipelines**, and **LLM-powered applications** to predictive analytics and data-driven business solutions. My interests include **Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, and scalable model deployment**.

Skilled in **Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, FastAPI, Docker, Streamlit, MLflow, and cloud-native development**, I continuously explore emerging AI technologies through hands-on projects and real-world implementations.

I'm always open to **collaborating on open-source projects, AI research, and innovative engineering solutions** that create meaningful business impact.
